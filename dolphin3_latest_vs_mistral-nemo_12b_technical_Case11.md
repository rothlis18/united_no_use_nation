The United Nations has not been universally successful for all people since 2000 and beyond. Its effectiveness varies greatly depending on the specific goals and contexts. While it has achieved some significant milestones, such as promoting peace and reducing conflicts in certain regions, it has also faced challenges like ineffective decision-making processes, lack of enforcement power, and the influence of powerful member states. The organization's ability to address global issues like climate change, poverty, and inequality has been limited by its structural and political constraints.
